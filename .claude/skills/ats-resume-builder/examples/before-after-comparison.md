# Before/After Example: Resume Optimization

This example shows how the ATS Resume Builder skill transforms a poorly formatted resume into an ATS-optimized version that passes applicant tracking systems.

---

## BEFORE: Non-ATS Friendly Resume

**Common issues:**
- Fancy formatting and graphics
- Inconsistent structure
- Missing quantifiable achievements
- Poor keyword optimization
- Weak action verbs

```

```

**Problems with this resume:**
1. ❌ Emojis and special characters confuse ATS
2. ❌ Fancy borders and formatting won't parse correctly
3. ❌ Vague bullets without quantifiable achievements
4. ❌ No specific technologies or impact mentioned
5. ❌ Weak action verbs ("did", "helped", "attended")
6. ❌ Non-standard section headers ("About Me", "Work History")
7. ❌ Missing critical information (full job titles, locations, months)
8. ❌ No LinkedIn or professional profile links
9. ❌ Unprofessional tone ("passionate", "loves coding")
10. ❌ Skills listed as "etc." instead of comprehensive list

## KEY IMPROVEMENTS

### 1. Formatting
- ✅ Removed emojis and special characters
- ✅ Used standard section headers
- ✅ Clean, simple layout that ATS can parse
- ✅ Proper date formats (Month Year - Month Year)

### 2. Content Quality
- ✅ Added quantifiable achievements (45% improvement, 100K+ users)
- ✅ Used strong action verbs (Developed, Improved, Implemented)
- ✅ Included specific technologies and tools
- ✅ Added business impact to each bullet point

### 3. Professional Information
- ✅ Added full contact information with LinkedIn

### 4. ATS Optimization
